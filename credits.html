Film and Video Production Resume — Available for Hire in New Jersey and the Tristate Area

Jay Franklin is a New Jersey based Director of Photography, camera operator, director, and editor with over 14 years of professional production experience. Jay is available for hire across New Jersey, New York, Connecticut, Pennsylvania, and nationwide.

  • DaBaby — Camera Operator, concert coverage, 24 Hours of Peace event, 2025

  • Steve Adubato, One on One — Camera Operator, television interview series, ongoing

Equipment and Capabilities

Camera systems including LUMIX S1, LUMIX S9, and GH5 with 6K open gate capability. Also experienced with Sony FX and A7 series, Canon C70 and R series, and Blackmagic Cinema cameras. Production formats include 4K and 6K narrative, live event multi-camera, broadcast and news format, gimbal and stabilized work, and drone operation with FAA Part 107 certification. Post production in Adobe Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve including color grading, sound design, and delivery to broadcast specification.

Jay Franklin is union-friendly, production-ready, and comfortable on set, on location, and under deadline. With the growth of film production in New Jersey and new studio development across Hudson County and Essex County, Jay is positioned as an experienced local crew member for incoming productions.
