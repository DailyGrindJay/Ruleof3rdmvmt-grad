The Film

A Story No One Thought to Tell.

Somewhere between the sidewalk and the storm drain, an ecosystem thrives unnoticed. Tumbleweave is a satirical mockumentary that follows Dr. La'ce Fruont, an independent urban naturalist who has dedicated his career to documenting what he calls "the most misunderstood organism in metropolitan America."

Through field observations, archival footage, and solemn voice-over, the film traces the complete life cycle of the hair weave, from its origins on the salon floor to its final resting place tangled in a chain-link fence on Broad Street.

With the sincerity of a nature documentary and the absurdity of something that absolutely should not be taken seriously, Tumbleweave asks the questions no one thought to ask: Where does it come from? Where does it go? And what does it say about the environments it passes through?

Tumbleweave Wins Three Awards at the International Movie Awards

Rule of 3rd Mvmt short film takes Best Indie Short and Best Dark Comedy. Lead actor Mike C. Scott receives Award of Merit for Best Actor in an Indie Film.

Roselle, New Jersey — Rule of 3rd Mvmt, the New Jersey-based film and photography production company founded by filmmaker Jay Franklin, announces that its short film Tumbleweave has won three awards at the International Movie Awards, including Best Indie Short and Best Dark Comedy as a Jury Award.

Lead actor Mike C. Scott received an Award of Merit for Best Actor in an Indie Film, recognizing the performance at the center of the film's satirical documentary conceit.

The film, a mockumentary following fictional urban naturalist Dr. La'ce Fruont as he documents the life cycle of the hair weave across metropolitan streets, blends the formal language of nature documentary filmmaking with sharp social observation and controlled absurdity. The result earned recognition across two competitive categories and drew praise for both its conceptual originality and its technical execution.

Tumbleweave is part of the Showrunners short film series, an original production from Rule of 3rd Mvmt. The recognition affirms Franklin's position as one of New Jersey's most distinctive independent filmmakers at a pivotal moment for the state's film industry, with major studios actively establishing production infrastructure across the region.

"Finding the story that was already there, that is the work," said Franklin. "Tumbleweave started as a question about what we overlook. These awards confirm that the question was worth asking."
