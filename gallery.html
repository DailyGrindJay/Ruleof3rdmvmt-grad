Jay Franklin | Director of Photography | Portfolio | New Jersey

Cinematography, Still Photography, and Film Work Across NJ, NY, and the Tristate Area

Rule of 3rd Mvmt is the portfolio of Jay Franklin, a New Jersey based Director of Photography and cinematographer with 14 years of professional experience in broadcast, narrative, commercial, and documentary film production. Jay serves clients and production companies across New Jersey, New York, Connecticut, Pennsylvania, and the broader Tristate region.

This gallery showcases still photography work spanning portrait, place, and object photography, alongside a selection of film and video work including documentary, short film, commercial production, concert coverage, and narrative filmmaking. Projects include work with public figures, live events, brand campaigns, and independent film festival selections.

Jay Franklin is available for hire as a Director of Photography, camera operator, and production crew member for film studios, production companies, advertising agencies, and independent productions operating in New Jersey and the Tristate area. Equipment includes LUMIX S1, LUMIX S9, and GH5 camera systems with 6K open gate capability.

With the growth of film production incentives in New Jersey and new studio development across Hudson County and Essex County, Jay is positioned as an experienced local crew member ready to serve incoming productions from day one.
